# Trauma ICU Simulator
A discrete-time simulator of a trauma intensive care unit, built for a Bachelor's Thesis. Patient arrivals, length of stay, nursing and physician workload, device use, mortality and readmission are measured from the Trauma SICU cohort of MIMIC-IV v3.1 (10,474 ICU stays). The simulator replays those stays under configurable bed, staffing and device capacity. Every figure in the interface is labelled by provenance: measured from the data, set by the modeller, or assumed. Only three constants are assumed, and they describe what shortage does to a patient (isn’t supplied by a record)
